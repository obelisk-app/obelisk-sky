## Sit down for mate

Walk up to any bench or stool and press <kbd>E</kbd> (on a phone, tap **Sit and drink mate**). Your character sits down and drinks mate. Press <kbd>E</kbd> again to stand up. It's the village's way of saying "I'm here, come talk".

<Clip name="mate-faces" caption="Mate on the plaza, passed from hand to hand." />

## The café and the parrilla

At the **café** you sit down with a coffee and a laptop, for when you're around but working. At the **parrilla** there's asado and cumbia playing, if your sound is on. Both work like the benches: walk up, press <kbd>E</kbd>.

## Campfires and voice calls

Every **voice channel** on the relay is an ombú tree with a campfire in front and log seats around it. Walk up and press **Join call** to talk. You come in with your microphone off, and the call bar turns it on. While you sit by the fire you hold a marshmallow over it, and a guitar plays nearby.

Calls are the same as in obelisk.ar: everyone in the call is equal, with voice, video and screen sharing.

<Clip name="campfire-sunset" caption="The campfire at sunset: where the voice channels meet." />

## The theater

The **theater** is a hall with a big screen and rows of popcorn seats. Pick a voice channel in its call bar and join. Whoever shares their screen puts it up on the big screen for everyone in the hall. Press <kbd>E</kbd> on a seat to sit and watch, and click the screen for **Watch mode**, where the film fills the page.

<Clip name="theater" caption="Watching together in the theater." />

## The calisthenics park

The park has bars for pull-ups, dips and push-ups, plus a running track around it. Stand by a bar and press <kbd>E</kbd> to train, or <kbd>2</kbd> for muscle-ups on the pull-up bar. Walk away to stop.

<Clip name="park-pullups" caption="Pull-ups and dips at the park." />

## Dance and emotes

Press <kbd>G</kbd> (or **Emotes** in the menu) and pick with <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> or <kbd>0</kbd>: dance, sit in the lotus pose, nod, fold your arms and more. Everyone sees it, and dances come with their own music. Walk off to stop.

<Clip name="dance-front" caption="Dancing on the plaza." />

## Play Chain Reaction

**Chain Reaction** is a game played over Nostr. Start one in a channel with `/play` (or the **Game** button in its chat) and a table appears in the village. Click it to play.

<Clip name="game-table" caption="A Chain Reaction table on the grass." />

## Zap people

Zaps are Bitcoin tips over Lightning. Connect a wallet once (a Nostr Wallet Connect link or a WebLN browser wallet), then zap someone from their card's **⚡ Zap** or by typing `/zap` in the chat. The zap flies across the village from you to them as coins, so everyone sees it. Sky also shows zaps sent from other apps, from their NIP-57 receipts.

<Clip name="zaps" caption="A zap flying across the village." />

## Landmarks to visit

Besides the channels' houses, the island has places of its own: **La Crypta**, the Buenos Aires Bitcoin community; the **nostr-wot HQ**; the **café**; the **parrilla**; **Liberture**'s house; the **theater**; and the **LABITCONF** stage. Some have official residents you can talk to.

<Shot name="landmarks" caption="La Crypta, the nostr-wot HQ, the café, the parrilla, Liberture and the theater." />

## Day and night

The island follows Buenos Aires time. At night the windows and the street lamps light up and the crickets come out. The sun button in the bottom bar switches between day and night.
