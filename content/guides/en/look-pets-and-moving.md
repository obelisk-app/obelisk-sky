## Open the look creator

The first time you come in, the look creator opens by itself to welcome you to the village. After that, press **your name at the top right**, then **Your look**. On the left you see your character turning, on the right everything you can change, and **Save** shows it to everyone.

<Clip name="look-editor" caption="The look creator: hair, outfit, a hat and a pet, then Save." />

## What you can change

- **Body**: masculine or feminine, and your skin tone.
- **Hair and beard**: bald, shaved, short, long, parted or in buns, plus a beard if you want one, in any colour.
- **Clothes**: shirt and trousers, an explorer outfit, a hood or no shirt at all, with colours for the top, the trousers and the shoes.
- **Hat**: a cap, a beanie, a top hat, a crown, a headband or a beret, in eight colours.
- **Pet**: two dozen animals, from a dog, a cat or a fox to a penguin, a bee or a little nostrich. You can give it a name.

<Shot name="look-creator" caption="The look creator over the village, with your character in front." />

## Saved on Nostr, not on a server

Your look is saved as a Nostr event of your own (a replaceable kind 30078). That means it **follows your key**: log in from another computer or your phone and you look the same. Your profile picture (kind 0) floats over your head, and **Edit profile** in the same menu changes your name, picture, banner, bio and lightning address.

<Callout>
On the guest island your look is kept in this browser only. Log in and save it again to take it with you.
</Callout>

## Pets

Pick a pet in the look creator and it follows you around the island. Everyone sees it, with its name if you gave it one.

<Clip name="pets" caption="Pets follow their owners around the plaza." />

## Walking, running and jumping

Walk with <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or the arrows, or click anywhere on the ground. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to run, or double-tap a direction key and keep it pressed. <kbd>Space</kbd> jumps, even while running, and holding it keeps you hopping. You can climb the obelisk's steps and cross the river on its arched bridges. On a phone, the thumb stick does all of this: push it to the edge to run.

## Ride a nostrich

Nostriches roam the island. Walk up to one **slowly** (they run from people who run at them), press <kbd>F</kbd> to climb on, then run to gallop. <kbd>F</kbd> again gets you off. On a phone, tap **Ride** when one is near.

## Swim

Walk into the river or the pond and you swim. Swim under the bridges and climb out on the other side.

<Clip name="swim" caption="Swimming under the bridge." />

## First person

Press <kbd>V</kbd> (or the eye button) to see the village through your character's eyes. Click to look around with the mouse, and press <kbd>V</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to come back. On a phone, drag to look around. It's the best way to walk into the landmarks and look up at the obelisk.

<Clip name="first-person" caption="First person, walking up to the nostr-wot HQ." />

## Finding your way

<kbd>N</kbd> shows or hides the minimap in the corner. Click it to open it big, then click a house to walk straight there. The house button in the bottom bar flies the camera out to show the whole island.
